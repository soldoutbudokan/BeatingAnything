# NBA pregame foul-risk proposal: do not fit the automatic Under rule

**The proposed directional correction is not identified by the available information.** A known risk of losing minutes does not show that FanDuel has omitted that risk. Main lines and prior scoring/minutes can already incorporate foul-related benching and disqualification. Subtracting another loss from such a baseline would count it twice. This proposal stops at design review; no foul values, prior exposure counts, target scores, fitted model or returns were read or calculated.

The current [NBA Rule 3, Section I](https://official.nba.com/rule-no-3-players-substitutes-and-coaches/) establishes disqualification on the sixth personal foul, with an exception when no eligible substitutes remain and a team would otherwise have fewer than five players. That is a sporting rule, not evidence about the bookmaker's forecasting implementation. The [independent design review](nba-foul-risk-design-review-2026-09-26.md) explains why the direction depends on which baseline has already been priced.

As a purely synthetic counterexample, let `X = m + r*d + epsilon - d*Bernoulli(r)`, with independent standard-normal `epsilon`, `r=.1` and `d=2`. Its mean remains `m`. Nevertheless, its chance of exceeding `m` is **0.5249267704**: a rare lower-tail loss can favor Over at a mean-adjusted line. This number is an independently checked mathematical illustration, not a forecast for any NBA player or a probability assigned to a bet. It rules out a logically automatic Under correction; it does not establish an Over edge either.

A defensible directional foul adjustment would need a prior-observable **change** in foul-loss probability relative to the risk already reflected in the quote, plus an independently supported remaining-opportunity baseline. A live foul-state test is a different mechanism and needs contemporaneous live prices. The retained 666-game price census is pregame. The last user-supplied entitlement record in the [September 19 acquisition plan](../docs/first-basket-oddspapi-followup-plan-2026-09-19.md) shows Live disabled; no newer evidence of access is available. No account endpoint, upgrade or live-history request was attempted here. This does not assert that no provider has a suitable archive.

The useful [identity-only census](nba-foul-risk-identity-feasibility-2026-09-26.md) maps 8,038 of 8,286 literal pairs to unique ESPN athlete IDs, retaining 248 unresolved pairs from eleven names. All 666 events retain at least one unique identity. It reads only identity columns across the full file, not target participation; it establishes neither prequote team membership nor prior role coverage. Source identities are reusable for a separately justified study.

The next distinct question is whether individual one-, two- and three-point scoring composition helps forecast **alternate-line tails when the distribution is anchored to the actual main-line probability**. Current [NBA Rule 5, Section I](https://official.nba.com/rule-no-5-scoring-and-timing/) supplies the scoring units. This is a distribution-shape hypothesis, not a foul counterfactual or a reopening of the closed main/alternate minimum-payoff screen. Price coverage and a coherent single model must be established before any performance join. Do not silently relabel the rejected foul proposal as that model.
