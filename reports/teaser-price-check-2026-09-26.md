## DraftKings settlement and applicability

The support page says any teaser containing an NFL selection uses its NFL table. Regular teasers with more than two selections reduce by the pushed/voided leg when no leg loses. A two-selection regular teaser, including one reduced to two selections, is refunded after a push/void if no selection loses. Super/Monster teaser rules differ and should not be used for the regular six-point ticket. Alternate lines are ineligible as teaser legs. [DraftKings support](https://support.draftkings.com/dk/en-us/what-is-a-teaser?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0010765).

[DraftKings Ontario Terms of Use](https://sportsbook.draftkings.com/legal/on-terms-of-use), marked updated **April 21, 2026**, identify Crown DK CAN Ltd and incorporate shared [general betting rules](https://sportsbook.draftkings.com/help/general-betting-rules/general-rules) and sport rules. This establishes an Ontario operation and a shared rules reference; it does not establish that the `en-us` support prices are executable in Ontario. The support page's linked [market-rules page](https://sportsbook.draftkings.com/help/general-betting-rules/market-rules) returned a navigation shell in this check, adding no Ontario price evidence.

## FanDuel Ontario settlement

The **30 July 2026** Ontario document names FanDuel Canada ULC and the Ontario sportsbook. Part B, General Rules §3 says teaser odds use a fixed payout chart. Section 18 says a pushed/void leg voids an entire two-team teaser; larger teasers remain active with the affected legs removed and payout recalculated. Unlike DraftKings' support wording, this clause does not explicitly qualify the two-team refund with “no losing selection.” Preserve that textual distinction; do not silently impose DraftKings' condition or claim that a loss-plus-push case was tested.
