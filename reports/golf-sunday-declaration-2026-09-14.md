# Sunday variance execution declaration — September 14, 2026

Recorded before this card's outcome comparison in the September 15 UTC continuation. The September 13 [card](../docs/hypotheses/golf-sunday-chasing-variance.md) fixes 2025, 4–6 versus 8–10 strokes behind, prior 20 rounds/minimum 10, one-stroke ability bins, exposure weighting, at least 200 exposed final rounds in at least 15 events, and a variance-ratio threshold of 1.15. These are unchanged. This note fixes the remaining implementation choices before calculating variances.

- Match the cached official 2025 schedule to ESPN by normalized event name (expand `pres.` to `presented`; `The Open` is `The Open Championship`) and validate dates. Retain all official individual four-round stroke-play tournaments, including multi-course events and the 2025 TOUR Championship, whose starting strokes were removed. Exclude team/match-play, Barracuda's Stableford, unofficial Hero World Challenge, and Q-School qualifying. The missing Grant Thornton source is outside the eligible team-free cohort.
- Baseline sources are all cached 2015–2025 PGA scoreboards. Use individual stroke-play completed rounds from earlier completed events, including complete rounds in shortened events. Exclude the same nonindividual/nonstroke formats and the 2019–2024 TOUR Championships with starting strokes. No age limit beyond the latest 20 rounds is added. All baseline events must end strictly before the current event starts; date metadata is retrospective and is not a historical publication clock.
- A complete round has exactly holes 1–18, integer positive strokes, and a round total equal to their sum. Ignore playoff periods beyond four. Prior ability is gross score minus the historical event-round completed-score field mean, averaged over the most recent 20 eligible rounds. This is the card's simple field adjustment; multi-course par/difficulty and field-strength confounding remain limitations.
- Compute the round-three lead from the minimum sum of complete rounds 1–3. Exposure is a cumulative gross-score gap of 4–6 inclusive; controls are 8–10 inclusive. Course rotations in the four retained multi-course events are complete by round three, so players then have equal scheduled course combinations. Retain all otherwise eligible round-three players in attrition even if their final round is absent. This retrospective reconstruction does not prove a live available leaderboard or participation status at the trigger.
- Final outcome is the complete round-four gross score minus the same event's completed round-four field mean, without further prior-ability subtraction. Bin frozen ability with `floor(ability)`, producing intervals `[k,k+1)`. A bin contributes only with at least two observed outcomes from each group. Report all omitted bins and observations.
- Use unbiased sample variances (`ddof=1`). Bin weight is the number of observed exposed outcomes in that bin. The primary statistic is the ratio of the exposure-weighted mean exposed variance to the exposure-weighted mean control variance, with identical weights on both. Sample gates count only exposed observations and distinct events represented in contributing bins. Weighted means and the separate group attrition are reported, not used to change the variance decision.
- Tournament-cluster bootstrap: sample the set of eligible 2025 events with replacement, preserving all players within each selected event; recompute bins, weights and the full statistic for 2,000 replicates using NumPy seed 20260915. Report the percentile 95% interval with linear quantiles, and valid replicate count. The threshold remains the point-estimate screen specified by the card, not a new confidence-interval gate.
