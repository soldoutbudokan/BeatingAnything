# G1 forecast wave-direction screen declaration

Declared September 14, 2026 (Toronto), after inspecting official tee-time metadata and before joining forecast wind to wave scores. No G1 forecast labels or wave-score comparisons have been inspected. This specifies the execution of [G1](../docs/hypotheses/golf-wave-draw.md). G10's variance comparison remains a separate card.

## Scope and the claim being tested

Use the same fixed 40 individual single-course 2025 PGA TOUR editions, rounds one and two, in [the official group inventory](golf-weather-group-inventory-2026-09-14.json). Reuse the frozen [operational GFS information set](golf-weather-declaration-2026-09-14.md), [venue mapping](../config/golf-weather-venues-2025.json), and G10's prepared prior-only ability/score joins. Acquire no additional years, forecasts, prices or alternative venues for this screen.

The measurable claim is **directional separation**: a wave identified as windier by a pre-event operational forecast subsequently scores at least +0.3 strokes worse, on average, after prior-ability adjustment. This uses the sign of the forecast difference. It does **not** predict a numerical stroke magnitude from wind speed: no coefficient mapping km/h to strokes is assumed, fitted or selected. The original card's numerical predicted-shift and forecast-revision claims remain untested. A directional pass can justify a separately declared calibration/price investigation; it cannot be inserted as a ready-made +0.3 adjustment into FanDuel markets.

## Freeze waves from tee schedules only

1. For each official event/round, use every published group and its scheduled UTC tee time, course ID and official PGA player IDs. Require a single declared course, unique player-round identity, finite timestamps, valid start tees, and all scheduled groups on one calendar date in the official venue timezone. A round spanning local dates is not treated as an ordinary morning/afternoon wave comparison.
2. Sort distinct tee times and locate the **unique largest adjacent gap**. It must be at least **60 minutes**. The groups through the earlier endpoint form the early wave; groups at or after the later endpoint form the late wave. Each wave must contain at least **25% of all scheduled players** in that round. Do not infer waves from scores, named players, a noon cutoff, or the size/sign of the forecast difference. Tied largest gaps, no qualifying gap, an imbalanced split, or missing metadata remain explicit schedule exclusions. No alternative split is tried.
3. This rule was selected from tee metadata only: ordinary within-wave gaps were 11–21 minutes; ordinary two-wave gaps were 169–213 minutes. The CJ CUP Byron Nelson R2 feed crosses two local dates and has an overnight gap. The fixed rule identifies 53 event-rounds across 27 events before weather and player attrition. Those counts are a preparation audit, not an outcome result.

Historical tee sheets fetched now may reflect revisions, withdrawals and actual tournament delays. They do not establish the exact grouping or tee time available at an old betting decision. This limitation applies even when the forecast itself is genuinely operational.

## Forecast exposure and direction

- Use the frozen operational 0.25-degree GFS run initialized at 00:00 UTC on the calendar day before the event's earliest R1 scheduled UTC tee time. Use that same run for R1 and R2. Each used object's retained LastModified must precede the earliest R1 tee time minus 12 hours. Keep run initialization, valid time, object/index clocks and current capture clocks separate. These checks do not independently prove historical public availability at a sportsbook quote.
- Reuse the completed weather acquisition and its full round window, from the floor of the first tee hour through the ceiling of the last tee plus five hours, inclusive. Require its entire scheduled round window to pass source identity, coordinate, clock and hourly-completeness checks. Do **not** filter on G10's `volatile` label or its 10 km/h range threshold.
- Each scheduled player inherits their group's **five-hour exposure**: the time average of forecast 10-metre wind speed, `3.6 × hypot(UGRD, VGRD)` in km/h, from scheduled tee time through exactly five hours later. Interpolate wind **speed** linearly between the hourly values and integrate that piecewise-linear curve exactly. Do not adjust duration using actual scoring pace or outcomes.
- Wave exposure is the player-weighted mean across **all officially scheduled players** in the wave, before ESPN score or ability attrition. Thus missing scores cannot change the forecast direction. The wave with the higher mean exposure is forecast harder. Every nonzero difference is used, including very small differences; there is no minimum forecast spread, quantile selection, alternative wind statistic, later run, or realized-weather substitute. An exactly zero difference has no forecast direction and is reported separately without a scoring contrast.

## Scoring, weighting and gates

- Reuse G10's prepared rows: unique full-name joins between PGA and ESPN, case/accent/punctuation normalization only; verified complete gross scores equal the sum of 18 unique holes; previous 20 complete field-round-centered rounds, minimum ten, from eligible earlier events ending strictly before this event. 2024 warms history. Current-event scores cannot enter its own ability; complete rounds from legitimate shortened earlier events remain eligible. No new name aliases or ability fitting.
- Join prepared rows to the frozen wave assignment by event, round, course and official PGA player ID; require group ID and scheduled time to agree. Each wave needs at least **two** distinct eligible scored players; otherwise the round is unresolved for scoring. Retain all exclusions and per-wave scheduled/joined counts. There is no trimming or extra skill binning.
- A round's late-minus-early outcome is the difference between mean `(gross_score − prior_ability)` in the two waves. Subtracting the same completed-round field mean from both waves would cancel exactly, so it is not estimated again. Both waves play the same course/round; its par also cancels.
- Multiply late-minus-early scoring by the sign of late-minus-early forecast exposure. This signed result is the forecast-harder-minus-forecast-easier scoring gap. Negative values count against the hypothesis and are never flipped or removed.
- Average contributing R1/R2 signed gaps within each event, then average those event values with **equal event weights**. An event can contribute one or both eligible rounds; report which. This prevents larger fields or events with two retained rounds from silently dominating the pooled gate. Also report the original late-minus-early wind and scoring differences, the raw forecast gap, and per-event results for diagnosis only.
- The sample gate is **at least 20 distinct events**, each contributing a round with both scheduled waves and both eligible scored waves. The fixed sport-side effect gate is a pooled signed difference of **at least +0.3 strokes**. An adequate sample below +0.3 kills this declared directional screen. Fewer than 20 events, no directional forecast, or undefined outcomes leaves it unresolved; do not relax the rule, change years or select stronger-wind events to rescue it.
- Report a deterministic **5,000-draw event bootstrap percentile 95% interval, seed 20260916**. Resample whole contributing events with replacement, retaining both of their original round contributions. Report the interval regardless of whether it supports the sign; the declared effect/sample gates are not replaced with a favorable interval rule. Repeated players, recurring venues, selection and imperfect skill control remain limitations.

## Execution controls and interpretation

`tools/explore_golf_wave.py --prepare` reads only the official tee sheets, inventory, existing prepared-score **file hashes**, and this declaration. It saves all 80 round schedule classifications and official wave membership; it reads no forecast values and computes no wave-score differences. Freeze the declaration, tee sources and prepared-score hash at this step.

`--compare` is not authorized until the root review of this contract and completion of the bounded shared weather acquisition. It must verify the acquisition has no pending hours or stop reason, accounts for all 80 rounds, and matches frozen input/output hashes. Permanent source failures and unknown venues remain missing observations after acquisition completes; a running, interrupted or capped batch is not a completed sample. Any changed declaration, group source, prepared-score source or acquisition manifest stops comparison.

A pass establishes neither a stable stroke forecast nor a book error. The same windier wave can differ in temperature, precipitation, course firmness, tee-time scheduling and player composition. Actual exposure can diverge from the five-hour scheduled window. No historical FanDuel quote clock, forecast revision response, market availability, settlement-aware expected value, return, alert or wager is established by this screen.
