# G10 weather covariance screen declaration

Declared September 14, 2026 (Toronto), after recovering operational forecast and group schemas, before classifying the cohort's weather or comparing same-group score differences. This supplies the unspecified implementation choices in [G10](../docs/hypotheses/golf-shared-weather-covariance.md); the original 10% effect, 300-group and ten-event gates stand.

## Cohort and information set

- Use the 40 official 2025 single-course individual medal events in the [identity inventory](golf-weather-group-inventory-2026-09-14.json), rounds one and two. These contain 3,390 published groups before player/history/weather attrition. The 2025 TOUR Championship has no starting strokes and is included. Multi-course, team, Stableford, qualifying and unofficial events are excluded before comparison. Later-round grouping depends on current scores, so this initial screen uses the opening two rounds only.
- Official PGA TOUR groups supply course ID, player ID and scheduled tee time. Join to ESPN by a unique normalized full name within the event, stripping case, accents and punctuation only. No fuzzy aliases. Retain unmatched, conflicting, incomplete and missing histories as attrition. Use the actual same-group membership, not synthetic groups.
- Freeze the GFS operational 0.25-degree run initialized at 00:00 UTC on the calendar day preceding the first round's earliest scheduled UTC tee time. Use that same run for both opening rounds. This is a historical pre-event information-set approximation, not an actual betting quote or proof of original tee-sheet publication.
- Require every used forecast object's retained LastModified to precede the first round's earliest tee time minus 12 hours. Store initialization, valid time, archive object modification, index modification and today's request/receipt clocks separately. An archival object modification clock supports the timing check but does not independently prove public availability at a particular sportsbook quote.
- Use individually source-identified course coordinates, with mapping-source attribution and ambiguity recorded. No city-center substitution. At the nearest GFS grid point derive wind speed as `3.6 * hypot(UGRD_10m, VGRD_10m)` in km/h. Use hourly forecasts from the floor of the first tee hour through the ceiling of the last tee time plus five hours, inclusive. This fixed five-hour window approximates play, without claiming actual hole-level exposure.
- A round is volatile only if the maximum minus minimum forecast wind speed over that full window is at least 10 km/h. Missing hours, invalid GRIB time/variable identity, unresolved coordinates, or a failed clock check leave a round unclassified. Do not replace it with a later run, observed weather, an alternative model or a shorter window. Acquisition must finish or explicitly report remaining missing inputs before scoring comparisons.

## Prior ability and comparison

- Recompute prior ability from the latest 20 verified complete rounds (minimum ten) in single-course individual medal events ending strictly before the current event starts. Use 2024 as warmup and 2025 sequentially. Exclude team, Stableford, starting-stroke formats, qualifying and unofficial events; retain complete rounds from legitimate shortened events. Subtract each historical completed-round field mean. No current-event round enters its own baseline.
- For each volatile course-round, retain each player's complete 18-hole gross score only if it equals the sum of the 18 unique hole scores. Form every unordered pair within an actual group. Controls are all pairs in different actual groups in that same course-round, whose tee times differ by at least 180 minutes.
- Orient each pair by ascending numeric ESPN player ID, independently of its score. Pair outcome is `(score_a - score_b) - (prior_ability_a - prior_ability_b)`. Group pairs by `floor(abs(prior_ability_a-prior_ability_b)/0.5)` within course-round. This controls the declared half-stroke ability-gap bins and removes the frozen mean difference.
- A cell contributes only with at least two same-group and two different-time pairs. Compute the ordinary unbiased sample variance separately in each cell, then average each variance using the same number of same-group pairs as weights. Report `1 - weighted_same_variance / weighted_different_time_variance`, both weighted variances and signed pair means. No alternate pairing distance, bin width, trimming or favorable-course selection.
- Count distinct actual groups with a contributing same-group pair, distinct events, players and both pair counts. Advance only at a reduction of at least 10%, at least 300 actual groups and at least ten events. An inadequate sample remains unresolved. A sufficient sample below 10% fails this screen.
- Report a deterministic 5,000-draw event bootstrap percentile interval (seed 20260915); resample whole events, preserving their course-round cells and all shared players/pairs. The interval is exploratory, with repeated-player/venue dependence and weather/tee-time selection limitations. It is not a test of FanDuel's formula.

No model pricing, FanDuel return, kernel, new confirmation allowance, alert or wager follows from this sport-side screen. A passing result needs actual timestamped derivative prices and verified settlement before a separate book-side test. G1's directional wave-scoring test is distinct and is not evaluated by this variance comparison.
