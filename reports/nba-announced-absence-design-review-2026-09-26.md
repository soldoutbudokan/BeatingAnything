# NBA announced-absence design review

Reviewed September 26, 2026. This is a prior-only design recommendation, not a declaration, cohort count, fit or return calculation. It uses the latest `NEXT-STEPS.md`, the [static review](nba-star-absence-static-review-2026-09-26.md), existing code, and the completed source/fixture audits. No new target performance values or network sources were read.

The mechanism is **all qualifying games with a previously established 20-PPG scorer explicitly listed Out**, including continuing absences. “All” refers to absence occasions, not every low-usage player listed Out. Keep the old role cutoffs of at least eight current-season appearances and at least 20 prior points per appearance. Do not add a first-absence, short-duration, position, opponent or remaining-teammate subgroup after coverage or results are known.

## Cohort and information set

Use the fixed literal FanDuel main-points pairs, half-point lines, decimal odds 1.20–6.00, overround 0–12%, and book/market ages 0–300 seconds at the original snapshot. Match ordered teams and fixture date independently, and require entry before the earlier provider/independent scheduled start. Preserve ambiguous or unmatched identities as exclusions. Select the first qualifying quote before inspecting status or historical scoring; if multiple same-time main lines remain for one player, reject ambiguity rather than choosing a favorable line.

Root's proposed information cutoff is **quote minus 60 minutes**. Search the predetermined six half-hour slots backwards from that cutoff, with the exact URL forms and request caps declared before acquisition. Stop at the first clock-qualified PDF even if the relevant team is not submitted, the fixture is absent, or nobody is Out. Do not search earlier reports for a desired status. Qualify the maximum of creation, interpretable revision/XMP/HTTP Last-Modified clocks and the printed minute's upper endpoint, not the filename hour. Require that maximum at or before the cutoff and no more than six hours before the quote. Preserve denials, missing files, ambiguous clocks and all stop reasons.

This identifies **Out in the selected lagged report**, not independently verified still Out at quote. No report status carries forward to another fixture. Within one report, reconstruct date, ordered matchup and team context across page breaks; collapse identical duplicate player rows, reject conflicting duplicate statuses, and treat `NOT YET SUBMITTED` as unknown. An unlisted person is not certified healthy. Current PDF assertions remain conditional historical evidence rather than contemporaneous publication receipts.

Known later prequote conflicts may exclude an event only through a declared mechanical scan of a frozen retained-source inventory applied to every event. Record the later source and its own clocks. Do not let ad hoc remembered news remove selected cases. Absent systematic newer-report acquisition, undiscovered overrides remain an explicit limitation; “no retained conflict” does not mean “no override.” A new acquisition declaration must preserve the stopped October denial and avoid repeated or alternate access to that request.

## Identity, team and prior role

Enumerate absent candidates from the selected report's explicit Out rows, then resolve stable person IDs from prior information. Never enumerate stars from target-game box rows. Preserve suffixes and require unique documented name matches; do not let target participation disambiguate a name. Report team/date is direct fixture-specific membership evidence for a listed player. A unique report identity can supersede an older appearance on another club; retain the discrepancy and chronology.

For an offered teammate, use the selected report's explicit fixture/team assignment when present. Otherwise use the latest chronologically available prior appearance's team as a declared proxy. Require a unique matching fixture club and flag this as inferred membership, not proof of no intervening transaction. Conflicting or unresolved evidence excludes the offer. Identity dictionaries and team assignments must not come from target boxes, final rosters or future appearances. Freeze each membership source and prior game ID.

Compute the star's current-season total points divided by played appearances using stable person IDs and independent UTC fixture order across all clubs, preserving the original player-season role definition. A zero-point played appearance belongs in the denominator; DNP and missing values are distinct. Team-specific counters cannot order a traded player's history. For a simple conservative availability convention, require every prior fixture's independent start plus 72 hours strictly before the information cutoff, and actual completion if retained. This is a declared publication-delay proxy, not a verified release timestamp. Do not import previous-season scoring to rescue early-season or season-long absences that fail eight appearances. Multiple qualifying Out stars create one exposed team-game, not duplicate teammate rows or an unplanned larger correction.

Retain all uniquely identified offered teammates except players themselves explicitly Out in the selected information set. Keep Questionable/Doubtful/Probable and unlisted offered players as such unless a different rule is declared now; do not later drop their losses or classify unlisted players as healthy. Forecasts concern the contract conditional on its settlement requirements. Missing target records cannot establish a void, and confirmed participation must never determine the forecast cohort.

## One fixed probability correction

For every exposed paired offer let `q = (1 / d_over) / (1 / d_over + 1 / d_under)` and use:

`p_over = logistic(logit(q) + beta)`, with `beta >= 0`.

Fit only this coefficient, with no fitted score distribution, role-size multiplier, line adjustment or grid. A concrete default objective is exposed-game-weighted Bernoulli log loss plus `0.5 * beta^2`: each distinct fixture gets total weight one across its eligible exposed offers, including both clubs if both are exposed. The derivative at zero is `sum(w * (q - y))`; if nonnegative, the convex constrained optimum is zero. Otherwise solve the unique positive root. No old hit rate or pooled-book return supplies the coefficient. This tests residual Over probability against the actual paired market; it does not prove injury causation or delayed bookmaker reaction.

Use the proposed fixed split: snapshots before January 1, 2026 for calibration, later snapshots for evaluation. **Also require every calibration label's declared availability bound strictly before January 1, 2026 UTC.** An entry-only split leaks late-December outcomes. Later prior histories may incorporate earlier evaluation-season games only after their declared availability time; the coefficient remains fixed. Freeze the source cohort and role calculation before exposing calibration labels, then freeze the fitted coefficient, every later probability and selection before target grading.

For odds `d`, compute `EV = p * 0.98 * (d - 1) - (1 - p)`. Keep the existing 3% threshold, fixed unit stakes and at most one selection per fixture, using maximum EV and a deterministic tie break. Computing both sides is harmless; the nonnegative Over correction cannot create an Under edge from a positive-margin paired market. If beta is zero or there are no qualifying later selections, preserve that terminal result and do not inspect later outcomes to choose another model.

## Coverage, grading and claims

Require at least 100 distinct exposed fixtures in calibration and 100 in evaluation before fitting, as root proposes. Count a fixture once even with multiple teammates, both exposed teams or repeated Out stars. This is a feasibility floor, not a power calculation: report distinct absent stars, teams, calendar weeks and concentration in repeated absences. A large group of teammate props cannot replace independent exposure. Do not broaden periods, roles or source rules to attain the floor.

After a nonzero frozen selection set, grade the complete frozen forecast cohort against the same paired-market `q`, with log loss and Brier differences, and grade the actual fixed selections after the haircut. Retain confirmed voids and unresolved rows separately; display unresolved-selection worst/best bounds. A genuine zero-point appearance is a settled outcome, not missing. Use game weighting consistently and account for common calendar and persistent absence shocks in uncertainty; a game-only interval may overstate independence. Preserve the existing 100 settled selected-game promotion gate separately from the 100 exposed-game coverage gate. Improved average loss and positive selected returns are separate requirements, not substitutes.
