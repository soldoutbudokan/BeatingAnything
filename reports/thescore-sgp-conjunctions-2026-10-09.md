# theScore Bet Parlay+ conjunction screen: every leg implied by two other legs (October 9, 2026)

**Result: 324 constructions on theScore Bet's Ohio board pay more when legs that the base already guarantees are stacked on it, 309 of them on uncapped quotes; 57 pay at least double the base and 23 at least five times.** The mechanism is the one PR 4 found by hand for McCaffrey: a two-leg base whose outcomes force a third stat (combined yards 90+ with rushing under 54.5 forces receiving of at least 36), on which the engine then multiplies in the forced leg's ladder rung and Over line as if they were new conditions. It is concentrated in NFL rushing/receiving props (163 of 305 accepted constructions, median ×1.63, maximum ×9.96) and WNBA stat combos (56 of 222, maximum at the odds cap), with a thinner version in NCAAF team totals (64 of 931, maximum ×4.18) and the English Championship (16 of 116). NFL team totals, MLB, La Liga, Serie A and Liga MX showed none.

**EV against FanDuel is not yet computed: the quote host is outside this container's network policy.** The fair reference for a two-leg base is FanDuel's same-game price for the matching four-cell partition, as PR 4 used. FanDuel's betslip prices a same-game parlay through `implyBets` on `sib.nj.sportsbook.fanduel.com` (captured from the site; anonymous, keyed by market and selection ids from the public event page); the own-build-parlay route `quoteChoices` on `qib.sportsbook.fanduel.com` is the other quote path. Both hosts answer this session with a CONNECT 403, so no cell was priced. `beating/fanduel_sgp.py` holds the quote client and `tools/devig_thescore_sgp_conjunctions.py --fetch` the full pipeline: for every instance it finds FanDuel's two-way markets for the base variables (or their constituents), enumerates the outcome space to choose the cell closest to theScore's base event, labels it exact, subset, superset or crossed, and would quote the four cells and devig them three ways. The [coverage report](thescore-sgp-conjunctions-fanduel-devig-2026-10-09.md) stands ready: 296 of 324 instances have a FanDuel fixture and 223 a FanDuel partition, 60 of them exact (55 NCAAF moneyline-or-spread with team or game totals at FanDuel's alternate lines, 5 NFL team-space), 122 supersets (NFL backs: FanDuel New Jersey posts rushing and receiving yards but no combined-yards market, so the nearest cell only bounds the base from above) and 41 crossed (NFL backs whose rushing line differs). WNBA (56) has no two-way player stat market on FanDuel New Jersey (ladders only, no combos), the Championship (16) is not on FanDuel's front page, and the soccer instances (17) need team goal totals FanDuel does not post for those games.

Every quote below was read from an anonymous Parlay+ draft that was eligible and error-free; refused drafts (suspended selections, correlated-market refusals, 38 in all) are excluded. No bet was placed; acceptance and limits are untested. ## 1. Method

[`beating/thescore_conjunctions.py`](../beating/thescore_conjunctions.py) turns every open selection into a predicate over a small integer outcome space: a player's rushing and receiving yards (combined yards derived); a player's points, rebounds and assists (the four combo stats derived); hits, home runs, bases and RBIs with their physical constraints; a game's home and away score for spreads, totals, team totals, moneylines and the soccer extras (both teams to score, double chance, correct score, handicaps). For every pair of selections from different markets of one space (up to 40 per player and 60 per game, favouring ladder rungs against main lines), the feasible outcomes are enumerated exactly; any other selection that holds on all of them, but not on either base leg alone, is a conjunction-implied leg. The stack is one implied leg per implied market, the highest threshold of each.

[`tools/screen_thescore_sgp_conjunctions.py`](../tools/screen_thescore_sgp_conjunctions.py) then quotes the base, the base plus each stacked leg alone, and the base plus the whole stack, on an anonymous Parlay+ draft, with the eligibility and draft-error checks from the corrected two-leg screen. 2,973 constructions were built across 209 pregame events (NFL, NCAAF, CFL, WNBA, MLB, NHL and ten soccer leagues; tennis, MMA, boxing and the minor leagues refuse Parlay+ outright). 2,767 stacks were quoted.

The single-leg quotes show the engine's rule is not consistent. Stacking one implied leg alone moved the price in 26% of NFL ladder cases and 45% of NFL Over-line cases, never in WNBA, and in 64% of spread cases. ## 3. What a devig needs

For a conjunction instance the winning event is the two-leg base; its fair probability is not a straight market. PR 4's approach stands: quote FanDuel's four cells for two FanDuel two-way markets (over/over, over/under, under/over, under/under), devig the four cells, and read the cell that matches theScore's base. `tools/devig_thescore_sgp_conjunctions.py --fetch` picks the cell by exact enumeration of the outcome space, so the result is an estimate when the cell is the base event, a lower bound when it lies inside it, an upper bound when it contains it, and an approximation when the lines cross; `--partitions <json>` takes the four prices read from the FanDuel site instead. Quoting needs `sib.nj.sportsbook.fanduel.com` allowed (the betslip's `implyBets`), which this session's network policy denies. FanDuel's combined-yards market is missing for the NFL backs in this screen, so those instances get bounds rather than estimates until FanDuel posts it or a second book with a four-cell same-game quote is used; WNBA player props were not two-way on FanDuel's New Jersey event pages at capture time.
