The [pinned NFL Market Tracker v1.1.1 archive](https://github.com/bobby-king3/nfl-market-movement-tracker/releases/download/v1.1.1/nfl_odds.duckdb) contains 23,780 FanDuel total rows in 11,890 two-sided snapshots across 286 source event IDs. The extra Rams–Washington Week 5 artifact cannot match the independent fixture audit and stays excluded. Valid pairs use one event, capture, scheduled start, ordered team identity, common book update and exact total line. Every capture is strictly before the earliest independent/audited/source start boundary; every book update is nonfuture. Matching uses the previous hash-pinned 285-fixture projection, with a fresh ordered-team and 15-minute schedule check.

The output preserves 157 stale pairs above 90 seconds and flags the earlier audit's age and 0–8% overround gates. Those flags identify 11,200 pairs; they do not select a betting cohort. Integer lines are supported with an explicit push flag, but all 11,357 retained lines are half-points and there are zero integer quotes. Scanning every book's source-start metadata tightened the prior moneyline audit boundary for 8 fixtures; the affected identities remain explicit in the inventory.

`captured_at` is the returned historical API snapshot; `bookmaker_last_update` is the provider's book-wide update. Original JSON payloads, simultaneous fills, jurisdiction and execution remain unverified.
