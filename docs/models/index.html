Projection models

Each model rates every player from the bottom up, using ball-by-ball or possession-level outcomes shrunk towards a role prior. A Monte Carlo game simulator then plays those ratings out thousands of times. Team totals, player lines and fair odds all come from the same simulated games. Each workbook opens like a spreadsheet: ratings tables, charts, a simulator you can rerun with your own lineups, and the method.
