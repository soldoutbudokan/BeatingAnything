# NHL power-play exposure and player-goal probabilities

Declared September 26, 2026 before computing these features against offered prices or grading this model. This is an exploratory new mechanism in the already inspected NHL archive, not an independent holdout or a modification of the closed shot-dispersion/consensus tests.

**Pricing hypothesis:** A power-play-dependent scorer's Over 0.5 goals price may underreact to an opponent's power-play shooting exposure conceded. More prior power-play shots faced should increase that component of expected scoring; fewer should decrease it. FanDuel may already incorporate the entire effect, in which case this correction can double-count it and fail.

**Prices:** Use the retained pinned November 26, 2024–January 25, 2025 archive's genuine paired FanDuel `player_goals` 0.5 line, same market update, valid American prices, 0–15% overround, and update in the 72 hours strictly before the earlier source/independent fixture start. Unique full normalized name→NHL ID and fixture mapping must pass the existing gates; exclude original debug game 2024020345 and unresolved identities. Earliest eligible quote per game/player, no target-participation filter, no extra or alternate lines. These provider timestamps lack original receipt/execution proof.

**Prior data and chronology:** SportsDataverse NHL boxes, regular-season game-ID type02 and independent schedule type R only. A prior game's independent UTC start+72 hours must be strictly before entry; current game always excluded. Missing schedule metadata excludes history and is reported. Do not treat local game-date midnight as UTC start. League constants use the completed 2023–24 regular season: `rho = skater PP goals / skater goals`, and `m = goalie-observed PP shots against / valid team-games`. Player history is the last 80 positive-ice-time skater appearances, at least 40, with valid integer goals and PP goals, 0≤PP≤goals. The latest available prior skater team must match one of the fixture teams; that identifies the opponent without target boxes. Opponent history is its last 40 valid regular-season team-games, at least 20. Arizona and Utah remain separate team codes; no retrospective franchise merge.

**Goalie aggregation:** `power_play_shots_against` contains saves/shots strings; use the denominator. Sum all appearing goalies for a team-game, preserving replacements. Zero-ice-time backups contribute zero only with valid zero counts; blanks are unknown. Require nonnegative integer split values with saves≤shots, finite valid ice time, reconciliation of even-strength/power-play/shorthanded splits to total goalie saves/shots, and shots−saves equal to the respective strength-specific/total goals-against fields. Exclude an entire malformed team-game and record it; do not partially count it. At least one goalie must have positive ice time; do not require exactly two listed goalies. This measures goalie-observed exposure, missing empty-net shots, rather than literal power-play duration or opportunities. A prior-only source check found 11 inconsistent 2023–24 team-games, motivating these uniform rules before any price-feature calculation.

The combined source leaves goalie positions blank. Normalize those rows to G only after their NHL IDs match goalie roles in the retained roster metadata; fail on an unexplained blank position. History additionally requires independent schedule state OFF or FINAL. These checks do not establish target-game participation. Prior-only source verification and a synthetic code review precede the first forecast run.

**Fixed probability rule, no target-label fitting:** `r=(player_PP_goals+10*rho)/(player_goals+10)` and `R=(opponent_PP_shots+40*m)/((n_opponent_games+40)*m)`. The ten-goal prior prevents sparse scoring histories from producing extreme shares; the 40-game prior assigns one full window of league-average exposure to the opponent. These are fixed conservative choices, not optimized strengths. Let proportional no-vig Under probability be `q=P(0 goals)`. Forecast `p_under=q**((1-r)+r*R)`, preserving q exactly when R=1. This follows an independent Poisson-component approximation with price-implied total intensity `-log(q)`, historical PP share r and opponent multiplier R applied only to the PP component. The decomposition is an assumption, not identification of the bookmaker's internal model. Do not use the same formula on higher goal lines, fit a coefficient, or search alternative windows/shrinkages after results.

At most one flat-unit selection per game, highest EV across eligible players/sides, decimal 1.20–6.00 and EV≥3% after 2% net-winnings haircut; lexical player/side tie-break. Assume full-game goals including overtime but excluding shootout; confirmed zero ice time is void, missing/invalid participation is unknown, and exact historical terms remain unverified. Preserve unknowns and full-cohort worst/best return bounds. Use 10,000 fixed-seed whole-game bootstrap resamples including zero-bet games; intervals do not correct for repeated player histories or earlier hypothesis searches.
