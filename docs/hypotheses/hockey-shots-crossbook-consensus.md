# NHL main shots: fixed cross-book consensus

Declared September 26, 2026, after price-only coverage and before this model's probability gaps, selections or grading. The archive's outcomes were already inspected for the completed dispersion experiment; this is explicitly exploratory and is not an independent holdout.

**Mechanism.** A single book's player-shot probability may lag or differ from a contemporaneous consensus of other operators. Distinct operators do not establish independent forecast errors. This is a paired-price forecasting test, with no sport-statistic regression or fitted weights.

**Source and target.** Use all 285 hash-verified raw JSON files at `ldinan-git/sports-betting-ops@42cf1f81bc302642ddcc9e88ce2e98c1057bc74d`, retained under `data/raw/nhl-shot-archive-2026-09-26/`. Only literal `player_shots_on_goal` main markets qualify. FanDuel needs unambiguous Over and Under at the same full player name and half-point line from 0.5 through 8.5, valid American odds and paired overround 0–15%. If multiple eligible FanDuel main lines exist for a player, choose the line whose normalized Under probability is closest to 0.5, then the lower line, before reference availability checks.

**Identity and time.** Use a unique full normalized roster name→NHL player ID without manual aliases. Match ordered teams to one independent regular-season fixture whose listed start is within one day. Use the earlier provider/independent start boundary. FanDuel market update must be within 72 hours before that boundary and strictly prestart. Exclude the original source-debug game `2024020345`. No prior-history minimum or realized participation filter applies.

**References and forecast.** Fix DraftKings, BetMGM, ESPN BET and Hard Rock Bet before probability comparisons. Require at least three reference books, each with a complete main-market Over/Under pair at the exact FanDuel player/line, valid American odds and overround 0–15%. Reference market update must be no later than FanDuel's update and no more than 300 seconds earlier. Use all qualified books from this fixed list. Normalize each paired Under inverse price by the sum of the two inverse prices; the forecast is the median of these normalized probabilities. Do not use alternates, interpolate lines, fabricate missing sides or move timestamps. No fitted weights or tuning.

**Selection.** Calculate each side's EV at the literal FanDuel price with effective decimal odds `1 + .98 × (decimal − 1)`. Require at least +3% EV and selected-side decimal odds 1.20–6.00. Choose at most one bet per game, highest EV first, then alphabetical player and side. Keep all predictions regardless of selection. Freeze the forecast CSV, selection, declaration, code and source hashes before this model's grading; refuse silent overwrite.

**Evaluation and uncertainty.** Compare all graded probabilities against normalized FanDuel probabilities using paired log loss and Brier loss. Report full-game shots including overtime under the stated settlement assumption; a confirmed zero-ice-time participant is void, missing/ambiguous outcomes remain unresolved. Never filter selections after outcomes. Report selected returns after the 2% net-winnings haircut, full-cohort loss/void/win bounds for unresolved selections, exposure, statuses and game-cluster bootstrap intervals (10,000 draws, seed 1729). Show a January 1 UTC boundary split descriptively; it does not restore untouched outcomes or justify subgroup selection.

**Limits and stopping.** Market update timestamps lack original capture/receipt and execution evidence; historical jurisdiction and exact void terms are unverified. There is no later-price series. This archive consists of irregular dates and already-inspected outcomes. Preserve null or negative results without changing the pool, timing, median, threshold, entry line or exposure.
