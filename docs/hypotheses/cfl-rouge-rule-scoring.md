# CFL rouge-rule scoring adjustment

**Sport / market / book:** CFL / team totals and alternative team totals / FanDuel; exact available lines remain unverified.

**Structural fact:** The 2026 rule removes the single point when an untouched kick exits the opponent's end zone. A returner conceding the ball in the end zone can still yield a single. Further field/goalpost changes are scheduled for 2027; do not pool those regimes.

**Predicted behavior:** Applying the 2026 scoring rule to otherwise unchanged 2024–25 kick trajectories removes at least 0.5 total points per game. This is a mechanical counterfactual, not a prediction of unchanged kicking or return decisions.

**Why FanDuel might miss it:** Unverified hypothesis: team-total tails retain a historical single-point component larger than the new rule permits. Public rules may already be fully priced.

**Trigger:** Published rule effective for the 2026 season; any price comparison must use an actual 2026 market and its current settlement rules.

**Fixed cheap screen, declared September 13 before scoring outcomes:** Complete 2024 and 2025 regular seasons. Identify scored singles after kicks that exited the end zone untouched, separating missed field goals, punts and kickoffs from returns/concessions. Require at least 100 fully classified single-point kicks and coverage of at least 90% of scheduled games. Kill the mechanical adjustment route if fewer than 0.5 points per game are removed; missing trajectories/touches or coverage leaves it unresolved. Do not classify every single as abolished or fit a generic total-score regression.

**Book-side test:** Actual same-time FanDuel paired team-total/alternate prices, including integer-line push treatment, under the applicable rules. A removed historical point does not establish price error, return or a tradable opportunity.

**Status:** idea; exact mechanical test unresolved.

**Result, September 13:** [The executed aggregate screen](../../reports/cfl-rouge-screen-2026-09-13.md) found 154 reported singles in 162 regular-season games. Conditional on those counts, the possible removal is 0–0.951 points per game, but no kick's untouched exit/concession was classified. Montreal's 2024 aggregate points are two below its fixture total, and a legacy endpoint requested for 2024 actually returned 2026; the latter was rejected before aggregation. This cannot pass the fixed screen or establish a scoring effect. Official fixtures end the 2026 regular season on October 24, 41 days away; keep this a secondary track, with new definitions needed for 2027.

**Calendar priority:** Included at the user's suggestion. The 2026 regular season is close to ending, so it must not displace the longer-runway NBA/rugby tracks; any 2027 continuation needs fresh rule definitions.
