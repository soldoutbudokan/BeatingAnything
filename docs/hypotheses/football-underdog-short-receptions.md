# Card 38 — Pregame short-target reception overs for substantial underdogs

Declared September 13, 2026 at 18:24 UTC, before this strategy's cohort was selected or its outcomes graded.

**Mechanism:** teams expected to trail may generate extra short-target receiving volume. A receiver whose recent targets are shallow may therefore beat a pregame reception line when his team is a substantial underdog. This is a pregame price rule, distinct from the completed live garbage-time usage card. Its observed sport-side percentage is not used as a fair probability.

**Fixed evaluation:** 2025 NFL regular season only. Use the newly recovered `firstandthirty/nfl-tools` FanDuel receptions CSV pinned at commit `e919241eb9fc17f057005348c7869a37b23e7675`, SHA-256 `795ae85ed74b0b1ad4971f8e2951a61704e79aff3138b760d3ec8fe802a2282d`. Independently match team names and scheduled start to the pinned nflverse fixture file, with a maximum 15-minute start discrepancy and exactly one match. All quote clocks must precede both reported and independently scheduled kickoff.

**Prior role:** exactly the three previous 2025 regular-season team games, before the prop snapshot; omit a team with fewer than three. Identify the quoted player by normalized full names on validated prior-game participation rows, requiring a unique stable player ID/team among the two fixture teams. Do not use the CSV's guessed player team or any current-game participation/outcome to select. Require at least ten targets with observed air yards, at least 90% observed target depth, mean depth no more than eight yards, and targets in at least two of those games. Exclude nullified plays and two-point attempts. Current-game receiving outcomes are unavailable to this preparation phase.

**Price:** exact FanDuel `player_receptions`, paired Over/Under at a positive half-point line, decimal prices each 1.2–6.0, pair overround 0–8%. Both bookmaker and market update must be no later than the archive snapshot and no more than 300 seconds old. Keep the original archive timestamp; do not repair future quote clocks. Reject ambiguous duplicate price keys.

**Underdog selection:** obtain same-team spread at least +6.5 from the latest eligible FanDuel spread snapshot strictly before the prop snapshot and at most six hours earlier. Use the existing pinned raw DuckDB; require two opposite team lines, valid paired decimal-converted prices with 0–8% overround, book update age 0–300 seconds, and identical pair identity/start metadata. No half-point constraint on the spread itself. Match the raw row's reported start and teams independently to the same fixture. Both spread and prop snapshots must be before the earlier of their own reported start and the independent fixture start. Do not use processed CSV spread guesses or a closing nflverse spread.

**Bet selection:** one earliest eligible Over per player/game. If multiple players qualify in a game, keep the shallowest prior mean depth, then the most prior targets, then lexical stable player ID. One unit risk per selected game. No fitted model or alternate thresholds.

**Grading:** freeze eligibility, selected prices and source hashes before loading current-game completion fields. Count valid completed passes credited to the selected receiver, including overtime, excluding nullified plays and two-point attempts. Verify actual participation from valid same-team participation rows or an unambiguous credited statistical play. Unknown no-participation/identity cases remain selected and receive pessimistic loss (−1 unit) versus void (0 units) sensitivity bounds; do not invent a DNP or delete the case. No push is possible on a half-point line. Wins earn `0.98 × (decimal_price − 1)` units, losses −1: the haircut applies to net winnings only. This assumes a conventional full-game reception market and void treatment for genuine nonparticipants; historical FanDuel jurisdiction/rule equivalence is unverified.

**Fixed gate:** at least 50 settled games, positive mean return, and a 95% game-bootstrap lower bound above zero (10,000 resamples, seed 1738). Below 50 settled games is unresolved regardless of ROI. At adequate sample size, failure of any return gate closes this specification; no threshold tuning or subgroup rescue. Unknown settlements must not reverse the conclusion under the pessimistic bound.

This is exploratory: 2025 outcomes were inspected for other project questions, and the publisher has analyzed its archive. It is not independent confirmation or an untouched holdout. Passing this screen would identify a research lead requiring separate validation and actual current price/settlement checks, without betting or alerts.
