# Observed quarterback replacement and short-target usage

**Registry ID:** 36. Distinct from the pregame-announced backup-QB card.

**Sport / market / book:** NFL / live remaining receiver receptions / FanDuel; exact offering and prices unverified.

**Structural fact:** An in-game quarterback replacement can change the immediate passing menu without the preparation available to an announced starter.

**Predicted behavior:** Prior short-target receivers on field when a new quarterback first appears receive more targets per subsequent offensive snap, after accounting for the ordinary change over the same game-clock and score state.

**Why FanDuel's price ignores it:** Hypothesis only: live reception projections may retain the preceding quarterback's target allocation while team expectations adjust. No actual stale price has been observed.

**Trigger:** First observed sole-QB change after ten consecutive offensive snaps with the same sole QB. Freeze eligible short-target receivers at that observed snap; count outcomes strictly afterward. No claim that the change was caused by injury or that the replacement remains in the game.

**Sport-side test:** Fixed 2023–2025 regular seasons, pinned nflverse PBP and FTN participation. Compare the frozen receiver pool's targets per team snap in the previous and next ten team snaps against unchanged-QB windows from other games, same team, season, quarter and score band. The [declaration](../nfl-observed-qb-switch-declaration-2026-09-13.md) fixes selection, controls and stopping. Require 100 matched replacement team-games and a >=20% adjusted relative increase. A smaller sample is unresolved; an adequate sample below the magnitude gate fails.

**Book-side test:** Actual FanDuel live receiver lines, accrued catches, executable prices, all settlement outcomes, and quote/state clocks after observing the replacement. Target allocation alone does not price receptions or establish an edge.

**Status:** sport-side dead under the fixed exploratory screen.

**Result:** [Executed September 13, 2026](../../reports/nfl-observed-qb-switch-2026-09-13.md): 124 matched replacement team-games clear the sample minimum, but adjusted short-pool targets/team snap rise only 10.12%, below the fixed +20% gate. Descriptive fixed-control game-bootstrap interval: −12.54% to +39.35%. All 411 later snaps with the previous QB remain. Known source disagreements are retained and disclosed. No year selection, future-persistence filter or extra sample is justified by this result; no FanDuel edge is established.
