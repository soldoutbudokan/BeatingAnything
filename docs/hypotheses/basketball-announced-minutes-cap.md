# Announced minutes cap

**Sport / market / book:** Basketball / returning-player points, rebounds and assists unders / FanDuel.

**Structural fact:** An explicit minutes limit reduces opportunities even if per-minute performance is unchanged.

**Predicted behavior (measurable):** A player with a team-announced cap <=24 minutes plays substantially fewer minutes than their prior unrestricted median >=30.

**Why FanDuel's price ignores it:** Unverified hypothesis: a prop is posted using usual minutes before a specific limit reaches all markets.

**Trigger (observable, timestamped):** Official team/coach announcement with a concrete cap before tip, followed by confirmed participation. Vague injury speculation is excluded.

**Sport-side test: data source, cost, kill criterion:** Official announcements and NBA minutes/box scores; free manual sample, <=1 hour. Compare actual minutes to stated cap and preabsence median. Kill if >=30 cases show <6 fewer minutes or reports do not give a usable limit; sparse trustworthy cases unresolved.

**Book-side test: what odds at trigger time, forward or historical:** Forward paired props before/after cap announcement and settlement rules. Record whether FanDuel withdrew or adjusted the offer before any actionable quote.
