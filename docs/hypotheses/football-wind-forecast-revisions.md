# NFL wind-forecast revisions and total prices

Declared September 26, 2026 before joining wind revisions to price changes or reading this test's football results. Exploratory mechanism; no change to the project's promotion gates. Other analyses have used this NFL archive, so it is not a globally untouched dataset.

**Mechanism:** Higher game-time wind reduces passing and kicking effectiveness. If the posted total incompletely incorporates an upward revision to the operational wind forecast, that revision should improve the probability of an Under after accounting for the paired current price. Falling forecast wind has the opposite predicted effect. This tests a weather-information adjustment, not the previously failed observed-wind long-kick card.

**Cohort and prices:** 2025 regular-season US games at statically open-air home venues, independently mapped by home team, expected stadium and `location=Home`; exclude retractable/fixed roofs and neutral/international fixtures. Use actual paired FanDuel half-point full-game totals from the pinned NFL DuckDB. Require a unique independent fixture, capture before the earlier independent/source start, one consistent book update aged 0–90 seconds, valid American prices and 0–8% overround. The first eligible capture 12–24 hours before that boundary is the one entry per game. No future endpoint or outcome is required for entry. Missing weather excludes a row before outcomes and stays recorded; no alternative run/station chosen based on results.

**Weather:** Use a fixed nearby GFS MOS station for each included venue, documenting the airport/stadium mismatch. The new run is the latest six-hour initialization no later than entry minus eight hours; the old run is exactly 24 hours earlier. Both must contain valid finite 0–98-knot wind forecasts spanning the identical independent kickoff-to-kickoff+3h window. Wind 99 is missing, not an extreme forecast. Average each window using piecewise-linear interpolation and trapezoidal integration of all in-window forecast knots and boundary values; no extrapolation. Feature `x=(new_mean_knots-old_mean_knots)/5`. The eight-hour buffer is a conservative availability assumption, not proof of original delivery; no realized weather or retrospective game roof field enters a forecast.

**Fixed probability model:** Let `q` be proportional no-vig Under probability from the entry pair. Forecast `p=sigmoid(logit(q)+beta*x)`. Fit the sole parameter on weeks 1–8 by minimizing summed binary log loss plus `0.5*beta**2`, constrained to beta≥0. At least 30 fully observed training games are required. No intercept, extra feature, sign reversal, threshold/window/station search or penalty tuning. If beta is zero, preserve q exactly. Every training game's conservative date+72h availability must precede every later forecast entry. Freeze source hashes, fitted beta and all week 9–18 predictions before grading those games. Preserve early-period fit statistics separately; do not pool them with later evidence.

**Betting rule:** On later-week entries only, use actual offered decimal odds, apply a 2% haircut to winning profits, and select the higher-EV side if EV≥3%, decimal odds1.20–6.00; one flat unit maximum per game, lexical side tie-break. Preserve zero selections and unknown results; half-point totals cannot push. Compare held-later-period log loss and Brier against q, and actual selected returns including unresolved bounds. Report week-block bootstrap intervals (10,000 resamples, fixed seed), recognizing the small number of independent weather/week blocks and broader search multiplicity. No edge is established merely by a positive return, fitted coefficient or forecast improvement in isolation.

**Price-response diagnostic:** The last eligible fresh paired prestart total, when available after entry, may describe subsequent line/price changes. Do not require it for the cohort, discard moved-line cases, substitute a later line into entry settlement, or claim verified closing value from sparse capture times. It does not select the model or bets.

**Status:** declared; acquisition and source mapping underway. No schedule, alert or wager is authorized by this experiment.
