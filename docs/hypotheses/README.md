For a cheap first test, use the exact exposure and control on its card, report sample size and the conditional difference, and stop if coverage fails. Percentage-point thresholds are exploratory triage choices, not profitability thresholds or statistical proof. A small sample is **unresolved**, not dead. Player/game clustering and obvious selection effects must be described; a conditional association does not prove the proposed cause. Do not build a fitted main-line model or new audit framework to rescue a card.

For the first four tennis cards, point-by-point data can answer the stated conditional question without odds. Check actual coverage, scoring rules, score reconstruction, and malformed/incomplete records before counting exposures. Reconstructed historical scores establish event order, not wall-clock trigger timestamps. Price testing requires independently timestamped state and quotes; it cannot be backfilled from point order.

Book-side comparison needs a matching market and line, both sides when available, settlement-rule compatibility, source and capture times, and contemporaneous game state. Remove vig using a declared method and retain spread between plausible methods. A Pinnacle move is a reference signal, not proof of fair value or executable FanDuel odds. Do not infer stale quotes from missing update timestamps, suspended offers, unmatched states, or aggregator lag. Archive raw observations under authorized source access.
