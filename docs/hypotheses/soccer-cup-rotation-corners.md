# Cup rotation and corner shares

**Sport / market / book:** Soccer / team corners and corner handicap / FanDuel.

**Structural fact:** Replacing wide attackers can change crossing and blocked-shot patterns without an equal change in match strength.

**Predicted behavior (measurable):** A cup lineup resting both usual wide attackers has lower team corner share than usual-lineup matches at similar possession opportunity.

**Why FanDuel's price ignores it:** Unverified hypothesis: corner markets inherit a broad match-strength adjustment without a role-specific lineup response.

**Trigger (observable, timestamped):** Official lineup publication excludes both wide starters, defined from prior starts/positions; identify exact competition and kickoff time.

**Sport-side test: data source, cost, kill criterion:** Official lineups and accessible match corner/possession data; football-data or FBref only where covered. Free, <=1 hour coverage check. Compare corner share by prior-defined rotation group and team/competition. Kill if >=100 matches show <5 percentage points lower corner share; insufficient role or publication data unresolved.

**Book-side test: what odds at trigger time, forward or historical:** Forward team-corner lines/pairs before/after lineup publication with reference match/total prices and unchanged market settlement rules.

**Status:** idea

**Result:** Not tested. Rotation may change the style in either direction; the predicted sign can fail.
