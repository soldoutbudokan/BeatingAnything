# NHL shots-on-goal count dispersion

Written September 26, 2026 before this model's forecasts or returns. Exploratory candidate; no change to existing confirmation/promotion gates.

**Market:** FanDuel player shots-on-goal Over/Under. Actual stored paired main-market prices in `ldinan-git/sports-betting-ops`, pinned `42cf1f81bc302642ddcc9e88ce2e98c1057bc74d`, November 26, 2024 through January 25, 2025. No substitute prices or inferred missing sides. Alternate ladders are not needed for this test.

**Mechanism:** A shot count varies with playing time, team possession and game state. A fixed-rate Poisson distribution understates dispersion when those exposures vary. If the sportsbook's count-to-price conversion does not fully account for a particular player's dispersion, replacing the Poisson shape at the same implied mean should improve tail probabilities. The Poisson assumption about the bookmaker is a hypothesis, not established provider behavior; this can double-count dispersion already priced.

**Fixed model:** For each paired half-point line, proportionally remove margin and invert its Under probability to a Poisson mean. For the named player, use the last 80 regular-season appearances in prior games, requiring at least 40, starting with the 2023–24 season. A game's date plus 72 hours must precede the market update, and the current game is always excluded. Compute `alpha_raw = max(0, (sample_variance - sample_mean) / sample_mean**2)`, with zero if the historical mean is zero. Multiply by `(n-1)/(n-1+40)`, cap at 1, and use a negative binomial with that alpha and the unchanged price-implied mean. Alpha zero exactly reproduces the market probability. No fitted coefficients, grid search, altered windows or threshold selection after results.

**Price cohort:** Preserve both sides from the same raw FanDuel `player_shots_on_goal` node. Require an unambiguous player identity in independently matched date/team game metadata, valid American prices, half-point line 0.5–8.5, overround 0–15%, and market update strictly before the earlier known start and no more than 72 hours before it. Where more than one paired line exists for a player, take the one closest to a balanced no-vig probability, breaking ties at the lower line. Never count duplicate copies as extra forecasts. Provider update timestamps lack independent receipt times; treat them as a historical timing assumption, not proof of an executable live offer.

**Selection and comparison:** Forecast and freeze rows before calculating target outcomes. Score both sides through the Under probability, comparing negative-binomial log loss/Brier to the unchanged proportional market baseline. At most one flat-unit bet per game: maximum forecast EV across eligible players/sides, decimal price 1.20–6.00, at least 3% EV after a 2% haircut to net winnings. Fixed lexical player/side tie-break. Preserve zero selections, unknown settlements and their worst-case bounds. Report all forecasts and selected exposure, whole-game bootstrap intervals, plus November–December and January results separately, split by start UTC at `2025-01-01T00:00:00Z` (thus including December 31 local evening games in the later period). January is a later chronological diagnostic, not proof of an untouched publisher-independent holdout. No prior project analysis used this archive; one November source-debug result was inspected and must be explicitly excluded from the new primary price test.

**Outcomes:** SportsDataverse publishes player boxscores derived from the NHL API, with stable NHL IDs, roster names and `shots_on_goal`. Source crosschecks must use ESPN `shotsTotal` (display label `S`), never ESPN `shootoutGoals` (display label `SOG`). A future-published retrospective file cannot certify its original data vintage. Source identity, timing and settlement limitations remain visible even if returns are positive. No scheduled collector, betting alert or wager.

**Status:** evaluated; no demonstrated betting edge. The declaration above was frozen in commit `dfaf458` before grading.

**Result, September 26:** [3,380 graded forecasts](../../reports/nhl-shot-dispersion-2026-09-26.md) improve pooled log loss by 0.001153; the later period's interval includes zero. Twelve frozen selections include 11 graded bets, 3 wins /8 losses and −4.3544 units after haircut, plus one unresolved. Full-cohort loss/win bounds are −44.62% to −25.34%, so even the missing bet winning cannot produce a positive return. Seven selections concern one player. No model or threshold retuning follows this result.

**Implementation clarification:** identity uses a globally unique full-name-to-NHL-ID crosswalk plus an independently matched fixture. It does not verify pregame player-team membership or filter on postgame participation. All 39 missing box rows, including one selection, remain unresolved. The originally frozen specification hash is retained in the freeze report and commit.
