# Backup quarterback and receiver props

**Sport / market / book:** Football / receiver yards and receptions props / FanDuel.

**Structural fact:** A quarterback change can shift pass depth and target allocation separately from overall team scoring expectation.

**Predicted behavior (measurable):** After an announced backup replaces a starter, deep-target receivers lose a larger share of receiving yards than short-target receivers.

**Why FanDuel's price ignores it:** Unverified hypothesis: the spread/team total reprices faster than receiver-specific role or depth assumptions.

**Trigger (observable, timestamped):** Official starter-out and replacement confirmation before entry; depth-chart rank alone is insufficient.

**Sport-side test: data source, cost, kill criterion:** nflverse play-by-play, prior air-yard shares and timestamped official availability reports; free, <=1 hour. Compare preclassified deep versus short roles around confirmed QB changes, separating injuries to receivers. Kill if >=100 eligible receiver-games show <15% relative yard-share shift; timely starter evidence missing means unresolved.

**Book-side test: what odds at trigger time, forward or historical:** Retained NFL archive inventory, then forward receiver props and paired team totals before/after news. Require matching players, lines and announcement times.

**Status:** idea

**Result:** Not tested; this does not resume the withdrawn generic NFL charting model.
