# NFL observed QB switch — fixed exploratory declaration

Declared **2026-09-13T18:14:52.570021+00:00**, before inspecting switch-cohort counts or outcome comparisons. New registry card 36; this is not the pregame backup-QB question or a rerun of garbage-time receptions.

- **Inputs and stop:** All 2023–2025 regular-season PBP from the existing pinned nflverse assets and corresponding FTN participation. Use their existing hashes. One batch only; no extra seasons, clock changes, role changes or subgroup rescue after results. Raw publisher files stay ignored.
- **Team snaps:** Non-nullified pass, run, QB kneel and QB spike plays with a recorded down and offensive team, excluding two-point attempts and overtime. Sacks count as pass snaps. Sequence by game and play ID. Missing/invalid participation is never filled from a later pass, catch, roster or eventual starter ID.
- **Observed QB:** An unambiguous eleven-player offensive list with eleven distinct valid GSIS IDs, eleven positions, matching possession team, and exactly one player assigned position QB. This establishes recorded historical position/participation, not a live feed's publication time. In dual-QB packages no sole QB is assigned.
- **Replacement trigger:** The first team-game snap where the sole QB differs from the identical sole QB present on every one of the preceding ten offensive team snaps. Require regulation game clock >=300 seconds, valid signed pre-snap score, and at least one qualifying short-target receiver in the observed trigger lineup. These conditions use only current and previous information. Mark the first qualifying QB-change snap even if its receiver pool is empty, so a later substitution cannot replace it. There is at most one exposure per team-game. No injury, depth-chart, starter or permanence claim is made.
- **Receiver pool:** At the trigger, freeze all WR/TE/RB/FB players whose role in the team's previous three regular-season games, strictly before the game date and within season, has >=10 targets with observed air yards, >=90% observed air-yard coverage, mean air yards <=8, and targets in >=2 of those games. The three prior games must exist. Use no current-game target/catch or future participation for eligibility. All later substitutions, returns of the previous QB and score changes remain in outcomes.
- **Windows and primary outcome:** For that fixed pool count targets (non-null receiver ID on a counted pass snap) over the preceding ten team snaps and the next ten team snaps, strictly excluding the trigger snap. If regulation ends first, retain the shorter or empty post window. The denominator is all team snaps in each window, including bench time. Report receptions for the same pool as a secondary descriptive quantity only; do not use their sign to rescue the target-rate gate. Previously completed play data can define the ten-snap baseline, but trigger-play outcomes do not enter either window.
- **Controls:** An otherwise eligible snap with ten immediately preceding snaps sharing its current sole QB. Keep the first eligible unchanged-QB snap per team-game/quarter/score-band. Score bands: <=-17, -16 through -9, -8 through +8, +9 through +16, >=+17. Match each exposure to control windows from **other games**, same team, season, quarter and score band. Later QB changes in controls remain; do not select controls by future QB stability. Multiple control windows from a game may enter different strata. A missing stratum remains unmatched.
- **Comparison:** In each matched stratum pool control targets and snaps separately before and after the control trigger. For each exposure multiply those two control rates by its corresponding pre/post snap counts. Sum observed and expected targets and snap counts. Primary adjusted relative change is `(exposed_post_rate / exposed_pre_rate) / (standardized_control_post_rate / standardized_control_pre_rate) - 1`. Zero required denominators make the effect unresolved. Report pre/post rates, raw relative change, standardized control change, counts by year, receiver concentration, actual post-window QB persistence and zero-target windows. Subsequent QB persistence is descriptive only and never filters the cohort.
- **Gate:** At least **100 matched QB-change team-games** and an adjusted relative increase >=**20%** passes this cheap sport-side screen. Below the sample minimum is unresolved, irrespective of a positive point estimate. Adequate sample below +20% fails this definition. Any interval uses 2,000 exposed-game resamples, seed 20260913, with control rates fixed; it is descriptive and omits control estimation uncertainty and repeated-team dependence. No confirmation/holdout claim.
- **Known input limitation:** The previous source diagnostic found fourteen completed passes in two 2025 games whose receiver is absent from the participation list. Retain these games and report overlap with this cohort; no inferred ID repair or outcome-driven exclusion. Report additional missing/invalid lineups and QB-position coverage.
- **Interpretation:** Short-pool identity and substitutions can change observed rates; score/quarter matching is coarse and opponents, field position and reasons for QB change are uncontrolled. Ten future team snaps have an unknown clock duration. No FanDuel line, available price, live latency, return or expected value follows from this sport-side screen.
