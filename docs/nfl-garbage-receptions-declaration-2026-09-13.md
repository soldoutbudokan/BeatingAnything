# NFL garbage-time receptions — fixed exploratory screen

Declared September 13, 2026 before comparing reception outcomes. Executes the existing [card](hypotheses/football-garbage-time-receptions.md), not a new fitted strategy.

- Fixed sample: all 2023–2025 regular-season games, with existing pinned nflverse PBP and FTN Data via nflverse participation. No extra seasons or cutoff changes after the result. Retrospective on-field records establish historical participation, not a live publication time.
- Trigger: the first scrimmage snap of a new offensive possession, in Q4 with at least 360 seconds left. A drive that began in Q3 is not a new Q4 possession. Exposure is offense score differential <=−17; control is absolute differential <=8. Freeze the on-field receiver at that snap, then count outcomes strictly **after** it, through the end of regulation. Excluding the trigger play prevents its observed lineup and outcome from being used as a presnap trading signal.
- Eligible receiver: WR, TE, RB or FB in an unambiguous 11-player offensive list at the trigger. Prior role uses the team's previous three regular-season games within the same season, strictly before the game date. Require at least ten targets with observed air yards, at least 90% observed air-yard coverage among those targets, mean target depth <=8 yards, and appearance as a target in at least two of those three games. Do not use current-game targets, eventual catches or subsequent participation to select the player. This is a prior short-target role, not proof of roster availability before the observed trigger snap.
- Keep only the first qualifying trigger per player-game across either category. Freeze eligible rows before counting remaining receptions. All later substitutions and changes in score state remain in the outcome. Do not require the player to remain on field or catch a later pass.
- Count non-nullified pass, run, QB kneel and QB spike plays with a recorded down as offensive team snaps. Sacks are pass plays. Exclude special teams, extra points, two-point attempts, nullified plays and overtime. Numerator is the player's completed receptions on these plays. Denominator is **all team snaps after trigger**, including the receiver's bench time; report total catches and remaining team snaps separately. An exposure with zero remaining snaps stays in the cohort with zero catches and zero denominator contribution.
- Match control windows from other games, same receiver and season, 180-second remaining-clock bin (360–539, 540–719, 720–899, 900), and 20-yard start-field-position bin using floor(yardline_100/20). No outcome-based matching. Missing strata are reported, not broadened after the fact. Compute each matched control stratum's pooled catches/team-snap rate, then weight that rate by the exposed team's remaining snaps. Compare pooled exposed catches/team snap with this standardized control rate. Also report simple catches/window and team snaps/window, with controls weighted by exposure counts.
- Existing gate: at least 200 **matched receiver-game exposures**, and >=20% relative reception-rate increase. Below sample minimum is unresolved; an adequate sample below the effect threshold kills this definition. A positive screen is descriptive, not evidence that FanDuel ignores it. Report exposure and control counts by year, missing coverage and player/game concentration. A 2,000-resample exposed-game cluster bootstrap (seed 20260913), holding control rates fixed, is only a limited descriptive interval, not full uncertainty or independent validation.
- Stop after this fixed batch. Do not tune depth, margin, clock, matching or outcome period. Require actual FanDuel live line, accrued receptions, executable quote and state clocks for any later price test. No odds or execution latency is inferred from historical play IDs.
