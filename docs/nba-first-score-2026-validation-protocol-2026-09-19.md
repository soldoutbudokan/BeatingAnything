## Purpose and scope

Test the **unchanged Card 42 conversion and betting rule** on another season/source. This is a conditional provider-state experiment, not an exact replication of the 2025 quote-receipt design. Preserve the original results and all advancement gates. No parameter search, new player subset, cutoff search or replacement bookmaker is permitted. The existing comparison family remains 16 because the same candidate is being evaluated; the extra source assumption cannot support a new confirmatory edge claim.

The dated Massachusetts [BetMGM November 2025 rules](https://massgaming.com/wp-content/uploads/BetMGM-House-Rules-11.24.25.pdf), page 25, exclude free throws from first field goal. FanDuel's [December 2025](https://massgaming.com/wp-content/uploads/FanDuel-House-Rules-12.16.25.pdf) and [March 2026](https://massgaming.com/wp-content/uploads/FanDuel-House-Rules-3.30.26.pdf) rules, page 30, include free throws in first basket and void a selected nonstarter. Their dates are supported by the regulator archive; the December PDF's printed approval date is a placeholder. These establish a conditional book-level interpretation. OddsPapi's normalized Player First Point label does not restore the original quote's jurisdiction or displayed market definition.

## Fixed source-state reconstruction

Use every originally frozen fixture, retaining missing histories and all exclusions in the denominator. Require its unique ESPN fixture match and regular-season type 2. Exclude Fridays by the earlier provider/ESPN scheduled start converted to America/New_York. Decision time is exactly **five minutes before the earlier verified scheduled start**. One fixture has a 12-minute start discrepancy; always use the earlier time.

For each book and player, take the latest history entry with zoned `createdAt <= decision`. Never backfill with a later entry. A latest inactive, null/invalid-price entry removes that player; never fall back to an older active entry. Conflicting entries at the same latest timestamp make the board unresolved. An unparseable timestamp makes the board unresolved because its ordering is unknown. Require exactly ten active, valid-priced, uniquely identified players at each book, with the same ESPN ID set. Duplicate provider IDs mapping to the same person make the board unresolved. More/fewer than ten is an exclusion, not a renormalization opportunity.

This assumes the latest recorded state persists until another provider entry. It does **not** establish historical execution, continuous feed completeness, original collection latency or a bookmaker update time. Record every entry age. Report, separately, the subset for which all twenty entries are at most 300 seconds old. This diagnostic does not turn `createdAt` into a verified bookmaker-update/receipt pair. No claims of satisfying the old freshness gate are permitted.

Player joins use unique full normalized names (ASCII accent normalization, punctuation/case removal, comma surname-first reversal), with no team-based rescue, suffix removal or outcome-driven aliases. Current provider team membership is never a historical feature. Link the same prior publisher player IDs by unique full names; unmatched players receive the fixed league prior. Use the exact previously pinned **2024** prior starter FG/FT totals, 100 pseudo-field-goals and `r=80/1319`; do not update prior rates from 2026 outcomes. Preserve original stable publisher IDs for tie breaking when available, otherwise prefix the stable ESPN ID.

Reuse `beating/first_score_wedge.py` unchanged: normalize BetMGM prices; convert with the fixed prior ratio; select at most one FanDuel bet with positive adjustment, decimal 1.20–26.00 and at least 5% modeled EV after a 2% winning-profit haircut. Also retain the original 0.99 probability reserve sensitivity and the selected bets' unconverted EV diagnostic. Freeze forecasts, source/code/protocol hashes and exclusions before reading independent scoring/starter columns.

The first positive scoring play must be in period one and its points must equal the combined scoreboard; use stable ESPN scorer ID and verify its unique name mapping. Free throws count. Missing/ambiguous identities or inconsistent first scores remain unresolved. Use box-score starter flags only for settlement, requiring ten unique starters, five on each fixture team, and a unique row/boolean starter flag for the selection. A verified selected nonstarter is void. Missing rows/flags remain unresolved, not presumed nonstarters. An unlisted scorer remains in the fixed 1% unlisted bucket for probability scoring.

Report the full 2026 validation cohort, without inventing discovery/replication splits within one month: selected stakes, wins, losses, voids, unknowns, ROI with unknowns as losses and as voids, reserve sensitivity, and paired log loss/Brier against raw BetMGM and normalized FanDuel. Keep every original selected stake in ROI denominators. Separately show the declared 300-second entry-age diagnostic. Use the existing week-block bootstrap only at eight or more calendar weeks; this fixed month cannot meet that inferential requirement. Do not combine source-incompatible cohorts to manufacture a narrow interval.

Negative results close this unchanged candidate on this source; positive small samples remain leads. Existing sample, predictive-skill, corrected uncertainty, exact market identity and prospective promotion gates still apply.
