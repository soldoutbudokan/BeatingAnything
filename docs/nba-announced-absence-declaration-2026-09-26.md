## Fixed probability model, chronology and decisions

For every exposed offered player, proportional no-vig market probability is `q=(1/over)/(1/over+1/under)`. Fit one nonnegative coefficient `beta` in `p=logistic(logit(q)+beta)` by minimizing earlier-period weighted binary log loss plus `beta^2/2`; beta is restricted to [0,2]. Each game has total weight one, divided equally among its settled active eligible player lines. No intercept, hyperparameter search or other features. Training entries must be before the January boundary AND their target game's completion-plus-24h bound must be strictly before that boundary. If this removes enough games to fall below 100 settled active training fixtures, stop without fitting.

Compute and freeze all later probabilities and selected sides before joining their target labels. The base market probability is retained for paired loss comparison. Only Overs can be selected under this directional mechanism. Net one-unit winning payout is `.98*(over_decimal-1)` and a loss is −1. Require modeled EV `p*(1+.98*(over_decimal-1))-1 >= .03`. Select at most one offer per game, highest EV then stable person ID then line. If there are zero later selections, close this exact model without later grading. Do not tune the penalty, split, direction, threshold, cohort, clock, membership proxy or prices after results.

Target settlement uses positive-minute appearance and actual points under a hypothetical full-game including-overtime contract. Half-point lines have no push. Confirmed DNP requires affirmative reliable evidence and reconciled team totals/minutes; absence of a row is unknown. Retain all unknowns; do not silently drop them.
