This is a cheap descriptive screen of the existing missing-creator card, not a confirmatory betting protocol. The card's threshold is unchanged: at least 100 exposed player-games and at least one additional assist per game.

**Inputs and cohort.** Use only the pinned 2024–25 regular-season player boxscores and schedule identified in `docs/season-runway-2026-09-13.md`. The cohort is every team-game with ID beginning `00224`. No additional season, threshold variant or chosen-team subset will be substituted after a result. Injury PDFs come from `akng8/nba-injury-scraper` commit `02cfe44f7453182eb4a29283285a7af64f5e3c9a`.

**Information cutoff.** Select one report per preceding calendar day: the latest filename hour present in the pinned tree. Validate its actual in-document timestamp and require that timestamp's calendar date to equal the preceding day. A filename is only a deterministic file-selection rule; it is not the information timestamp. Require the injury row to identify the target game date, matchup, team and creator. Missing files, ambiguous dates, unresolved names and `NOT YET SUBMITTED` teams are exclusions. Do not infer that a missing player row means Available. This conservative preceding-day rule tests role redistribution, not an intraday news response or stale quote.

**Prior roles, frozen before joining statuses.** For each team-game, use the ten most recent team games dated strictly before the report's calendar date. This deliberately excludes all games on the report date because scheduled tip and completion times are absent. Require ten complete prior team boxscores. A candidate must appear in the most recent prior team boxscore, play in at least eight of those ten games, average at least 20 minutes across the ten games, and have no other-team appearance during that window. Rank candidates by total assists across the ten team-games, then by NBA person ID. The top candidate is the creator and must average at least five assists; the second candidate is the secondary and must average at least 2.5 assists. No current-game starts, minutes or assists enter this selection. If a selected player has a known other-team appearance after that window but before the target date, exclude the pair as a roster change.

**Exposure and baseline.** Exposure is an explicit `Out` row for the preselected creator in that preceding-day report. Exclude a secondary explicitly listed `Out` or `Doubtful` in that same report, before accessing the current outcome; a missing secondary row stays unknown. The comparison baseline is that same secondary's assists and minutes in the prior-role window's games where the selected creator actually played positive minutes. Require at least five such prior games. These are observed prior creator-present controls, not inferred pregame Available reports. Use the mean of those controls for each exposure, then average current-minus-prior differences equally across exposed player-games. This same-player baseline limits player-level mix but does not control opponents, trends, joint absences or why the creator was absent.

**Outcomes and missingness.** Retain preselected secondaries with zero minutes and assists, including absent player rows when a complete team boxscore is verified against schedule team assists and minutes. Verify the sum of player minutes within one minute of the schedule total and an exact team-assist total. Incomplete team boxes are missing, not zero. Report actual creator participation despite the preceding-day Out report as a diagnostic; do not exclude it using the eventual outcome. Report current and baseline assists, minutes, aggregate assists per 36 minutes, number of secondary zero-minute games and sample attrition. A rate difference alone cannot pass the expected-assist screen.

**Uncertainty and decision.** Report a descriptive team-cluster percentile bootstrap interval using 5,000 draws and seed 1729; it is not multiple-testing-adjusted evidence. At least 100 exposures plus a mean assist increase of one or more passes only the card's sport-side screen. Below 100 is unresolved. At 100 or more and below one, this specified route fails. Preserve the result without changing role thresholds, expanding seasons or reversing the predicted direction. No historical FanDuel prop quotes are in these inputs, and no outcome here establishes an edge.
